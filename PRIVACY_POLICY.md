# Privacy Policy for RDMS Ultimate

**Effective Date:** October 14, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 14, 2026  

## 1. Introduction
RDMS Ultimate ("we", "our", or "the Application") is an offline-first Document Management System developed to help users organize, digitize, and back up their documents. This Privacy Policy explains how our application accesses, uses, stores, and protects your information when you connect your personal Google Drive account.

## 2. Google Drive Data & Scopes
When you connect your Google Account, RDMS Ultimate requests access to the following scoped permission:
- **`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file`**: Create new documents, read files created by this application, and upload document backups to your personal Google Drive.

### What We Access:
- RDMS Ultimate **ONLY** accesses files and folders that were explicitly created or uploaded by RDMS Ultimate.
- We **DO NOT** access, view, modify, or delete any of your other private personal files, photos, or documents on your Google Drive.
- We do not read your Gmail, contacts, or calendar.

## 3. How We Use Information
- **Backup & Synchronization**: Document metadata, PDF files, and attachments you choose to back up are securely uploaded directly to your own Google Drive storage.
- **Client-Side Storage**: OAuth 2.0 access tokens are stored securely on your local device using encrypted platform storage. Tokens are never transmitted to unauthorized third parties.

## 4. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance
RDMS Ultimate's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements:
- We do not sell user data to third parties.
- We do not use Google Drive data for advertising or marketing.
- We do not use your data for training generalized AI/machine learning models.

## 5. Data Retention & Revocation
- **Revoking Access**: You can disconnect your Google Account at any time directly in the app via **Cloud Settings > Putuskan Akun**, or revoke permissions through your [Google Account Security Settings](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
- **Data Deletion**: When you revoke access or delete your local documents, all cached local credentials are automatically erased from your device.

## 6. Contact Us
For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your data privacy, contact the developer at your registered support email.
